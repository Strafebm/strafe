ABOUT STRAFE, AND WHAT WE STAND FOR:

I am Winterfell - diabolic incarnation of the crimson moon, and this is the official site to my Black Metal band, STRAFE. True, raw Black Metal brought to you from Central America. I, vocalist and main guitarist of the band, along with secondary guitarist, Mars, are the two souls of dual decay behind the rituals practiced to bring STRAFE's demonic sound to life, like the mangled abomination of Victor Frankenstein. Unfortunately, as per complications regarding our equipment, STRAFE has not been able to fully record a two-person demo. As of the making of BLOOD OF BATTLE, STRAFE is a solo effort lead by me, but things will change!

STRAFE stands for one thing and one thing only: the complete erradication of all that is right and moral. We believe in total HEATHEN ANARCHY! And with our music, we believe to gather those true and loyal to the Black Metal underground, and join us in the total erradication of Communist posers across the globe.

...RECLAIM YOUR PAGAN IDENTITY - MAKE BLACK METAL HATEFUL AGAIN! HAIL WOTAN, HAIL STRAFE!
