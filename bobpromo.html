About us

Blood of Battle, STRAFE's debut:

STRAFE is currently amidst completing their 1st demo - BLOOD OF BATTLE. This release is an ode to all the fallen soldiers who have given their lives in battle - to the very idea of death, and WAR. 20+ minutes of pure heathen madness, mixed with the diabolic screams of the damned, and blast beats so loud, it'll TEAR YOU APART.
Despite being a two member band, this demo has been an entirely solo effort recorded by Winterfell, throughout the span of 2 months. After this demo releases, we are planning to begin organized rehearsal once again, and record a new tape alongside a potential new drummer for the band. After this, we will also open our official Bandcamp page, for any who are interested in STRAFE.

For now, BLOOD OF BATTLE is expected to be near completion, with 5 tracks fully mixed and ready to go, and a sixth track nearing its final stages of mixing. Below is the planned song order for the demo, but be aware that it could change later on, and new songs could be recorded:

------ Blood of Battle ------ 1. Intro / Blood of Battle ☑ 2. In Dark Wooden Coffins I Rest ☑ 3. Mjolnirritter ☑ 4. Talos, the Bronze Giant ☑ 5. Winter Wolves Howl Up to the Crimson Red Moon ✎ 6. Transylvania Uber Alles ☑
